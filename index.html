Economist

Benjamin Lerch

I am an economist at the Swiss Federal Finance Administration and am also affiliated with the Università della Svizzera italiana. My work focuses on applied economic questions related to public finances, climate change, demographics and automation.

I hold a PhD in Economics from Università della Svizzera italiana and an MSc in Applied Economic Analysis from the University of Bern. In 2022, I was a visiting researcher at the Department of Economics of Stanford University's.
