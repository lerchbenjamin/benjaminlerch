Economist

Benjamin Lerch

I am an economist at the Swiss Federal Finance Administration. My work focuses on public finance and applied economic questions related to climate change, healthcare, demographics and automation.

I hold a PhD in Economics from Università della Svizzera italiana and an MSc in Applied Economic Analysis from the University of Bern. In 2022, I was a visiting researcher at Stanford University's Department of Economics.
