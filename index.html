About

Applied economics with a public-policy focus

I am an economist at the Federal Finance Administration (FFA) in the Swiss Federal Department of Finance. My work focuses on long-term fiscal challenges and applied empirical questions, including climate change, healthcare, demographic change and technological change.

I hold a PhD in Labor and Macroeconomics from Università della Svizzera italiana and an MSc in Applied Economic Analysis from the University of Bern. In 2022, I visited Stanford University's Department of Economics as a researcher.
